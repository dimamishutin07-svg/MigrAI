# How to get an e-visa?

## Check e-Visa Eligibility

### Checking Eligibility for an Electronic Visa

Before submitting an application, a foreign citizen must check whether an electronic visa of the Republic of Kazakhstan is available for their country of citizenship and purpose of travel.

Depending on the purpose of entry, the legislation provides for the issuance of the following types of single-entry electronic visas:

official;

investor;

business;

private;

tourist;

medical treatment visas.

The specific visa category is determined in accordance with the current Rules for the Issuance of Invitations and Visas of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

Who Can Obtain an Electronic Visa

Electronic visas are issued to citizens of countries included in the list approved under Appendix 11 to the Rules for the Issuance of Invitations and Visas of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

A special condition applies to citizens of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan: the issuance of a single-entry electronic business visa under category B3.

Before submitting an application, applicants must check the current list of eligible countries and available visa categories on the official Visa and Migration Portal of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

An electronic visa is issued on the basis of an invitation from the inviting party that has been approved in accordance with the established procedure.
